# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful and welcoming experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or point of view.

## Our standards

Examples of expected behavior include being considerate, giving and receiving constructive feedback, acknowledging mistakes, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, sexualized language or advances, publishing another person's private information, and sustained disruption of project discussions.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently exclude contributors for behavior they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Report issues privately to the project maintainers. A public contact address will be added before the first public release. Maintainers will respect the privacy and safety of the reporter.

This code of conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html
